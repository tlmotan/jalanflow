# 0015 — Map cards for a quick look (trial)

- Status: trial · 2026-11-24. Built only on the trial page (`web/trial.html`). The site (`web/index.html`) is unchanged and still follows `design/project/DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §1. This becomes "accepted" only if Tim adopts the trial.
- Context: On the site, tapping a marker or a road replaces the text column with its detail, so a reader loses what the column was showing just to learn a name and a lane count. `design/project/DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §1 says "No tabs and no map popups". Tim's brief for the trial (2026-11-24) asks for compact cards anchored to the map, and says the ban on popups is lifted for these previews only.
- Decision (trial page):
  - **A tap on a marker or a road opens a small card beside it**, pinned to that place as the map moves. The panel keeps showing what it was showing. With a mouse, pointing at a marker previews its card; it goes when the pointer leaves unless it was clicked. Roads need a click.
  - **The card is a preview, not a second panel.** A lane drop shows what it is, its name, lanes in → out, one sentence, and whether lanes are guessed. A road shows its name, lanes and what it can carry. "View details" opens the full detail in the panel, where everything else lives.
  - **Nothing computed before the analysis.** A road's card says "Bottleneck", "Nearly full" or "Room to spare" only after "Run the analysis" (decisions 0004, 0011).
  - **Observed and computed stay apart, by shape and words** (as decision 0014 does on the site). A confirmed jam is a red ringed dot; its card is headed "CONFIRMED JAM" and says "Seen by a local driver." The bottleneck is a thick red line; its card is headed "BOTTLENECK" and says "Worked out by the analysis, not measured."
  - **The card's top label is in capitals**, as in Tim's mockup (asked for twice, 2026-11-24). It is the one exception to "no all-caps labels" in `CONTENT.md`. The capitals are a style: the text stays in sentence case for screen readers.
  - **One card at a time.** Another tap replaces it. A click elsewhere, Escape, a direction switch or running the analysis closes it. Escape closes the card first, then goes back a view.
  - **The card never sits on its marker.** MapLibre's own popup picks the side with room and is offset from the point; it is restyled to the dark shell (272 px wide, 14 px padding, 8 px radius).
  - **The card sits on the map wherever the map can hold it** (at least 320 × 300 px), phones included. On a smaller map the preview takes the place of the sheet below it, with "View details" and "Close" (44 px targets), and the map pans just enough if the tapped place would be hidden. First built as "phones never get a card"; Tim did not find the preview in the sheet (2026-11-24).
  - **The camera follows the view, not the last zoom** (Tim's brief, 2026-11-24). The start, the result and "How this works" frame the whole drive; the lane-drop list frames every lane drop; a road or lane-drop detail frames that one feature (no closer than zoom 14.5), unless it is already in view at zoom 13.5 or closer, when the reader's camera is kept. Moving to another view frames it again, so a zoom meant for one view never carries into the next. Within a view the reader's own pans and zooms are kept through resizes. A card moves the map only when the card or its place would be cut off, and only by that much; hover previews never move it. Padding keeps roads clear of the zoom buttons and the credit line; the panel and the phone sheet sit beside or below the map, never over it, so the map's own box already leaves room for them. View changes ease for 350 ms; resizes jump; reduced motion jumps everywhere.
  - **The keyboard route is unchanged**: the lane-drop list and the "Show it on the map" button open the panel detail directly, since map features cannot take focus.
- What this would replace if adopted: "no map popups" in `design/project/DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §1, for these previews only. "No card" for the text column and "no unnecessary cards" elsewhere still hold.
- Consequences: web only, no output format change, no new dependency (`maplibregl.Popup`). The card and the panel detail are built from the same data, so the two cannot disagree.
