# 0017 — The phone sheet floats over a full-height map

- Status: accepted · 2026-11-25 (Tim: "Adopt, then build", after the prototype on `design.fig` page 8).
- Context: On phones the panel sat below the map as a sheet and never covered it (`design/project/DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §1). Every view changed the sheet's height, so the map changed size with it. Measured at 390 × 844: the map was 401 px on the start and 480 px on the result, but 298–308 px with More detail, the list or a detail open. Each change re-framed the map, and below 420 px no map card fits (decision 0015), so taps fell back to the sheet. At 375 × 667 the detail map was about 224 px.
- Decision:
  - **The map fills the screen under the bar and never changes size.** The panel is a sheet fixed to the bottom of the screen, over the map's lower part, with a top radius, a hairline and a shadow.
  - **The camera works in the part still showing.** `sheetCover()` in `web/src/main.ts` measures how much of the map the sheet covers; the camera padding, the card room check and the card nudge all add it. The credit line sits just above the sheet (`--sheet-cover`). Desktop is unchanged: there the panel is beside the map and the cover is 0.
  - **Three heights, all from content:** collapsed (the top of the view and More detail), expanded (up to 64 % of the screen, always leaving 220 px of map; only the sheet scrolls, overscroll contained) and a tapped place (its card in the sheet when the map showing is under 420 px; otherwise the card sits on the map, as before).
  - **Buttons first, a drag as well.** More detail / Less detail (stays at the top while the findings scroll), Back (stays at the top in the list and detail), Close. Escape works as before.
  - **Dragging the grabber** (Tim, 2026-11-25: "add the sheet drag gesture too"). A 28 px strip across the top of the sheet, kept at the top as the sheet scrolls. The sheet follows the finger down and gives up to 16 px going up. Let go after 48 px, or faster than 0.5 px/ms, and it acts: up opens the findings; down closes them, closes a quick look in the sheet, or goes back from the list, a detail or How this works. A tap opens or closes the findings. Only the grabber drags, so scrolling the findings never moves the sheet. It is hidden from screen readers, because the buttons do everything it does. The camera measures the sheet's layout box, not the dragged one, so it frames for where the sheet comes to rest.
- Update 2026-11-26 (decision 0018, agreed, not built): three heights by adding a collapsed one that keeps the title line, the not-live-traffic line and a handle that is a real button; a drag only changes the height, so it no longer goes back or closes a quick look; and the sheet's height no longer moves the camera, which replaces "the camera works in the part still showing" above. Until it is built this decision describes the site.
- What this replaces: "It never covers the map" in `DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §1, for phones.
- Not done: browser Back does not close the sheet (the site uses `replaceState`, so Back leaves the page, as before).
- Consequences: web only (`style.css`, `main.ts`). No output format change, no new dependency.
