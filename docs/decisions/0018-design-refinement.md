# 0018 — Design refinement: each part of the page has one job

- Status: **agreed in concept · 2026-11-26 (Tim). Not built.** Frames, sizes and timings are not approved yet. Nothing under `web/` changes for this before Tim approves the frames in chat (`design/WORKFLOW.md` step 5). Until then the site behaves as decisions 0015 and 0017 describe.
- Context: The sheet, the cards, the detail, the list and "How this works" had grown to overlap: the list opened the detail directly, the result carried the lane strip in full, and on phones every change of the sheet's height re-framed the map, so a reader who had found a place lost it by opening More detail. Tim agreed seven refinements in a design discussion and handed them over on 2026-11-26. The handover was written against public `main`; it was checked against the working tree the same day, and the points below are where it meets what was already decided.
- Decision:
  - **One job each.** The lane-drop list finds and navigates. The map card explains one place. The detail investigates it. The result draws the conclusion. "How this works" explains the method. A lane diagram is not repeated in the list.
  - **The phone sheet has three heights**: collapsed, summary (the default; today's closed sheet) and expanded. A drag snaps between them and a button does the same. Heights come from testing on real viewports and safe areas, not from the 7 / 35 / 85 % of the sketches.
  - **Collapsed is not empty.** It keeps the view's title line, the not-live-traffic line and a handle that is a real, focusable button. `DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §12 ("Not live traffic" on screen in every view) and "nothing needs the drag" (0017) both still hold.
  - **A drag only changes the sheet's height.** Back and Close stay buttons. This replaces 0017's "a drag down goes back from the list, a detail or How this works, and closes a quick look".
  - **The sheet never moves the camera.** Dragging, snapping, opening, closing and scrolling the sheet do not pan, zoom or re-frame the map, and do not change a padding that shifts it. The camera frames once for the summary height. It moves only when the reader asks to go somewhere: picking a row, a marker or the bottleneck, changing view or direction, or the map's own controls. This replaces "the camera works in the part still showing" in 0017 and the phone part of "the camera follows the view" in 0015. `MOBILE.md` §6 (fit the selected feature into the part not covered) is met by the selection, not by the sheet.
  - **The result, in order**: the direction and the most the roads can carry, with its unit and the not-live-traffic line; the limiting road and a short reason; the one-more-lane what-if. The key, replay, flow and the two links stay findable.
  - **"View lane layout" is the lane strip, closed by default.** The bottleneck is a road, and a road carries only a lane count in the data; the lane diagram needs the joining roads and sides that only lane drops have. So the disclosure under the bottleneck opens the strip of `DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §13, drawn from the data as now.
  - **The what-if is two labelled bars**, today and with one more lane, from the same zero on one scale, each with its number and unit. No gain shows equal bars and says so, with the reason from `fixes.json` (`why_no_gain`), never a guessed one. No baseline or a zero one shows no bars. It is labelled a test in the model, not advice to build. `DESIGN.md` §6 prefers a direct comparison where two numbers are enough; the bars are kept because the no-gain case reads faster as two equal bars than as a sentence. The numbers stay beside them.
  - **Assumptions and difficulty go under "About this estimate"**, inline. The qualifier that limits the claim stays beside the claim, outside the disclosure.
  - **A list row goes to the place and opens its card**; "View details" on the card opens the detail. This replaces "the keyboard route is unchanged: the list opens the panel detail directly" in 0015 and `DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §8. The keyboard route stays whole another way: choosing a row moves focus into the card, where View details and Close are buttons, and closing returns focus to the row. No step needs the map canvas. Deep links to a lane drop still open its detail.
  - **Open, to settle on the canvas:** on phones with the list open, less than 420 px of map shows, so the card would fall into the sheet in place of the list just tapped. The frames must show where the card goes there before this part is approved.
  - **The road detail gains a bar**: what the analysis sends against what the road can carry, with the percentage and both numbers. Only after the analysis; a road with no or zero capacity says so instead. A full road is not called the bottleneck unless the data marks it (`is_cut`). A lane drop's detail shows its lane diagram. Assumptions and sources sit in disclosures; the Street View, Google Maps and OpenStreetMap links stay.
  - **"How this works" leads with one drawing and three steps**: map the roads, estimate what each can carry, find the bottleneck. The drawing is a made-up network, labelled as one, not the corridor. The limits come next, in the open: it is capacity, not demand or travel time; some lanes are guessed; the per-lane values are not calibrated. The notes from `manifest.json` and `lane_drops.json` stay, under a disclosure. No claim is added that the data or the pipeline does not make; simulated results are mentioned only once Phase 1.5 publishes them.
  - **Motion: quiet when informing, livelier when the reader acts.** Cards, disclosures and view changes take about 120–200 ms on the existing timing tokens. The sheet follows the finger and can be interrupted. The analysis replay, the flow and asked-for map moves keep their character. Reduced motion, Skip, Replay, the hidden-tab pause and decision 0011 are unchanged.
  - **Card labels stay in capitals.** The exception in `CONTENT.md` (decision 0015) stands.
- Found while checking (a defect, not a design change): changing the direction through the address bar while the analysis runs does not stop the run. Reproduced 2026-11-26: with `#outbound` set mid-run, the heading and the switch said "to Kota Kemuning" while the panel went on counting "Route n of 8", the routes towards Petaling Jaya (the other direction has 12). The direction buttons do stop the run, and held up under rapid switching during and after a run. The fix is in `web/` wiring only, with a test that fails first; the capacity model is not touched.
- What this replaces, once built: the points named above in 0015 and 0017, and `DESIGN_BRIEF.md` §1 (sheet, result order), §5 (camera on phones), §8 (list opens the detail) and §13 (strip always open). Each carries a note pointing here.
- Consequences: web only. No output format change, no new dependency, no change to the model. `docs/USERFLOW.md`, `screenshots/flow.mjs` and `screenshots/motion.mjs` change with the screens. Before publishing, a few first-time visitors are asked what the number measures, where the corridor is limited, whether it is live traffic and what the what-if means; no claim of usability or accessibility testing is made before that is done.
